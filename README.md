# Life &amp; Finance Lens

Life &amp; Finance Lens helps you understand how your life choices impact your financial future.
Visualize how life events shape your financial journey over time through an intuitive timeline view.

Explore how major life events affect your finances, understand the long-term consequences of
financial decisions, map out future life events, and make informed decisions about your life
journey.
